# Code of Conduct

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Procera a harassment-free experience for everyone. That holds regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes:
- empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:
- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to **conduct@procera.dev** (placeholder until the domain is registered; until then, contact any maintainer privately). Maintainers who are not involved in the incident will review and investigate every report promptly and fairly, and respect the reporter's privacy.

Responses follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement ladder:
1. **Correction:** a private written warning.
2. **Warning:** consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban.**
4. **Permanent ban.**
