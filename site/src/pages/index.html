Who it's for

Built for startups to multinationals, in every department

The same product scales from a starter profile with sensible defaults to an enterprise profile with stricter floors. Settings are layered, so a department can tighten a rule without a fork.

Startups
Install the employee handbook pack, publish it and have 20 people acknowledge on their phones within 15 minutes. That run is an automated test.
Growing companies
Departments, managers and locations from a CSV import; announcements to one office with a confirm-by date and manager escalation.
Large organisations
Custom roles scoped to departments and collections, separation of duties on reviews, a hash-chained audit log and per-workspace encryption keys.

Open source and self-hosted

Your policies and your evidence stay on your servers

Procera is Apache-2.0 licensed, with no telemetry by default. It needs PostgreSQL and an SMTP server. Valkey is an optional cache, and files sit on local disk today, with an S3-compatible backend next.

Apache-2.0

Use it, change it, run it commercially. Contributions are DCO signed-off.
