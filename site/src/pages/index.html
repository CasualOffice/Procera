07 · Open source and self-hosted

Your policies and your evidence stay on your servers

Apache-2.0, with no telemetry by default. Procera needs PostgreSQL and an SMTP server. Valkey is an optional cache, and files sit on local disk today, with an S3-compatible backend next.

  • PostgreSQL 16+
  • SMTP
  • Valkey (optional)
  • ClamAV (recommended)
